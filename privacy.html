Neuron Snake

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Neuron Snake handles information when you use the app and related website.

Information We May Process

  • Gameplay and app state stored locally on your device
  • Leaderboard or feedback information that you choose to submit
  • Purchase status for the one-time Remove Ads upgrade
  • Advertising-related information needed to deliver and measure ads

Third-Party Services

Neuron Snake may use third-party services to support app functionality, including analytics, ads, purchases, and backend infrastructure. These may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • RevenueCat for in-app purchase management
  • Supabase for app-backed features such as leaderboard or feedback flows

Purchases

If you buy the Remove Ads upgrade, purchase processing is handled through Apple and RevenueCat. Neuron Snake does not directly store your full payment card details.

Ads

The free version of the app may show interstitial ads. If you purchase the Remove Ads upgrade, ads are disabled for that user entitlement.

Data Retention

Locally stored game state remains on your device unless you remove the app or clear the data. Third-party services may retain submitted data according to their own policies.

Your Choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time
  • You can request purchase restoration through the app
  • You can avoid submitting optional leaderboard or feedback information

Contact

For support questions, visit neuronsnake.com/support.html or open an issue at github.com/amyleesterling/neuron-game/issues.
